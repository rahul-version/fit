Welcome to the Administrative Suite

Use the left-hand navigation sidebar to manage different parameters of the gym operation:

  • Members: Add manual user files, change subscription plan dates, deactivate expired memberships, and delete accounts.
  • Inquiries: Read contact form requests and track follow-ups (New → Contacted → Resolved).
  • Trainers: Maintain trainer coach records dynamically displayed on the website front end.
  • Testimonials: Verify and approve reviews before showing them publicly.
  • Attendance: Monitor facility entry records logs.
